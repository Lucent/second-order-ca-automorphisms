\begin{abstract}
This note is a supplement to \cite{PaperB}. For a second-order automaton $F_f$ with $f$ a left-permutive elementary rule, the two-copy window graph $\Gamma_{w,M}$ follows both configurations of a pair that differ behind a front moving at the speed of light, on the $w$ cells behind the front and the $M$ cells ahead of it. We determine its eventual image: it has $2^{3w+2}4^M$ states and every walk of length $\lceil w/2\rceil$ ends in it. For the rules $30$, $45$, $75$ and $135$ we prove that it is strongly connected for all $w$ and $M$. Strong connectivity is reduced to the holonomy of closed walks at each layer behind the front; for $30$, $45$ and $75$ this is read off from explicit periodic histories, and for $135$ it is obtained from a single closed walk of odd length by an induction on the depth.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{tc:sec:intro}

The proofs of \cite{PaperB} follow one configuration along the front, through the graphs $\Lambda_M$. Here we follow both configurations of a front pair, through a \emph{two-copy} window graph $\Gamma_{w,M}$ that also records $w$ cells behind the front, and we determine its eventual image: its size, the number of steps after which every walk has entered it, and, for the rules $30$, $45$, $75$ and $135$, its strong connectivity (Theorem~\ref{r30:thm:window} and Proposition~\ref{pm:prop:twocopy}). Strong connectivity is reduced to a statement about the holonomy of closed walks at each layer behind the front. For $30$, $45$ and $75$ this holonomy is read off from explicit periodic histories, one family for all depths. For $135$ we know no such family, and the holonomy is obtained from a single closed walk of odd length by an induction on the depth (Proposition~\ref{pm:prop:odd}). No theorem of \cite{PaperB} uses these results. We use the notation of \cite{PaperB} throughout, and references of the form ``Section~3 of \cite{PaperB}'' are to that paper.
