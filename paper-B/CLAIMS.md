# Paper B: statements and the computations that accompany them

*Fronts at the speed of light: rigidity of permutive second-order cellular automata.*

The main theorems of this paper (Theorem 3.1 for rule 30, Theorem 5.1 for the elementary permutive rules, the theorems of Section 4 for permutive rules of any radius and of Section 6 for the classes of 63 and 187) are proved **by hand**. Three propositions of the paper are **computer-assisted** and are marked so there: Propositions 4.40, 4.41 and 4.42 (radius 2 and 3). Appendix A ("Computations") of the paper specifies them (A.2), the computer-assisted statements made in remarks (A.3) and the confirmations of results proved by hand (A.4). The supplement (`tex/supplement.pdf`, the two-copy window graph) has one computer-assisted proposition, Proposition 8.1 (the lift certificate); its Section 10 ("Computations") specifies it and the confirmations of the supplement's results. No theorem of the paper uses the supplement. The tables below list every computation that Appendix A of the paper or Section 10 of the supplement mentions. Statement numbers are those of `tex/main.pdf`, and those marked "of the supplement" are those of `tex/supplement.pdf`.

- Paths are relative to `paper-B/`. Commands are run from `code/`, those of `code/sat/` from `code/sat/`, those of `code/halfradius/` from `code/halfradius/`.
- The programs need Python 3 with NumPy; those marked **SAT** also need PySAT (`python-sat`).
- Tier **quick**: run by `make check`, in a scratch copy, with the output compared with the committed file (`diff`) or with the expected lines given here (in total 3.3 min on one core, peak memory 0.65 GB). Tier **full**: run by hand; `make full` prints the commands.
- Every command of both tiers was run with Python 3.12, NumPy 2.5.4 and PySAT 1.9.dev21 and gave the output stated; times and peak memory are for one core of the machine used for the checks.
- Rules of radius 2 are named by the function g: in `code/` by the hexadecimal truth table, in `code/sat/` by the string `gtab` (`code/README.md` explains both conventions).
