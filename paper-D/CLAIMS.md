# Paper D: statements and computations

*The 88 second-order elementary cellular automata: a classification by automorphism group.*

Every statement of the paper that depends on a computation, or that the appendix confirms by one, with the program, the command and the expected output. Numbers are those of the compiled paper (`tex/main.pdf`); "A.1" etc. are the subsections of Appendix A (Computations) that specify the computation. Paths are relative to `paper-D/`; a command is run from the directory named in the column "directory".

Status:

- **computer-assisted**: the statement is the output of the computation;
- **confirmation only**: the statement is proved by hand, and the computation is not used in a proof;
- the classification itself (Theorem 1.1) is **by hand** from the results of the companion papers (I1 to I14 of Section 1.2) and the computer-assisted statements below.

Tier: **quick** rows are run by `make check` (about 90 seconds on one core in all, at most 460 MB) and compared there with the committed logs; the time and memory given for them were measured on one core. **full** rows are long runs whose committed log is named; their times and memory are those recorded in the logs, in the READMEs of `code/` or in Table 5 of the paper, and `make full` prints their commands. All programs in `code/census` except `classes88.py`, `classify_r4.py` and `identify_r4.py` need the package `python-sat`.
