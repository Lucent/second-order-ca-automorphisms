# Paper E: statements and computations

*The cellular automata that commute with Rule 54, and rigidity of the second-order automaton 94R.*

Every statement of the paper that depends on a computation, or that the appendix confirms by one, with the program, the command and the expected output. Numbers are those of the compiled paper (`tex/main.pdf`); "A.1", "A.2.4" etc. are the subsections of Appendix A (Computations) that specify the computation. Paths are relative to `paper-E/`; a command is run from the directory named in the column "directory". The READMEs of the subdirectories of `code/` give the complete expected output of every command.

Status:

- **computer-assisted**: the statement is the output of the computation (Appendix A.1 and A.2);
- **confirmation only**: the statement is proved by hand, and the computation is not used in a proof (Appendix A.3).

All other statements of the paper are proved by hand.

Tier: **quick** rows are run by `make check` (under three minutes on one core in all, at most 600 MB) and compared there with the expected output or the committed log; their times were measured on one core, and where no memory is given it is under 250 MB. **full** rows are not run by `make check`: their committed log is named, or their expected output is in the README named; times and memory are those recorded in the READMEs and logs. `make full` prints the commands. "SAT" marks the rows that need the package `python-sat`.
